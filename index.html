  • arXiv 2026Preprint

    Different Tasks, Different Timesteps: Temporal Task-Aware Quantization for Diffusion Language Models

    Ofek Avivi*, Eliron Rahimi*, Avi Mendelson, Chaim Baskin · *equal contribution

    T²Quant gives each denoising step of a diffusion LM its own activation precision, per task, from a label-free signal. It improves three recent quantizers on every model at the same 4-bit budget, by up to 6.7 points.
